🔀 Channels

Channels are the only way packets get sent — there's no default destination anymore. Each channel catches packets matching specific ports and sends them to whatever multicast IP:Port you set, e.g. a "Snapshot" feed on one port going to one group, "Incremental/TBT" on another. At least one channel is required — any packet that doesn't match one of your channels is not sent anywhere.
